# Prompt: concise startup pitch deck

Use the [master prompt](master-presentation-prompt.md) contract. Generate a 10-slide investor pitch:

1. Company and one-line promise.
2. Customer problem.
3. Initial target user and use case.
4. Product workflow.
5. Why the approach is distinct (avoid unsupported competitor claims).
6. Market framing using only supplied evidence.
7. Business model and clearly labeled assumptions.
8. Go-to-market experiment.
9. Milestones, funding use, or open risks.
10. Specific closing ask.

Audience: early-stage investors. Tone: candid and evidence-aware. Use concise action titles, one message per slide, and speaker notes for assumptions. If market size, traction, pricing, or customer data were not supplied, label the corresponding content as a hypothesis or request the missing evidence rather than inventing it.
