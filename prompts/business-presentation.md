# Prompt: decision-oriented business presentation

Use the [master prompt](master-presentation-prompt.md) contract. Turn the supplied business question and evidence into a 6–10 slide decision deck for senior leaders.

Lead with the decision required, then establish context, evidence, options, tradeoffs, recommendation, implementation steps, risks, and next action. Use comparison slides for genuine alternatives. Put caveats and definitions in notes. Do not invent benchmark values, costs, or stakeholder positions. Every factual claim should be traceable to material provided by the user; preserve unknowns explicitly.
